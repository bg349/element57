Why phosphate matters

Chlorine kills algae. It doesn't take away its food.

Algae needs sunlight, warm water and nutrients. The nutrient most pools have too much of is phosphate. It arrives with lawn fertilizer, leaves, pollen and dust, and some tap water and pool products add more.

When phosphate is high, algae grows back the moment chlorine runs low after a hot weekend or a storm. Most services respond with another heavy shock. The pool clears, the phosphate stays, and the cycle repeats.

We remove the phosphate. Lanthanum chloride binds with it to form lanthanum phosphate (La³⁺ + PO₄³⁻ → LaPO₄), a solid that doesn't dissolve, so the filter catches it and algae can't use it.

Phosphate removal works alongside chlorine, not instead of it. Your pool still needs a sanitizer to be safe to swim in.

What a phosphate treatment involves

  1. Test

    We measure phosphate in parts per billion, plus chlorine, pH and alkalinity.

  2. Treat

    We dose lanthanum chloride for your pool's volume and reading, with the pump running.

  3. Filter

    The water may haze for a short while as phosphate binds and the filter collects it.

  4. Clean and retest

    We clean or backwash the filter, vacuum any residue, and test again to confirm.

What your number means

General guidance. Many pool professionals aim to keep phosphate below 100 ppb.

ppb

PhosphateWhat it means
Under 100 ppbLow. Algae has very little to feed on.
100 to 500 ppbEnough to feed algae. One treatment usually brings it down.
500 to 1,000 ppbHigh. Expect blooms whenever chlorine slips.
Over 1,000 ppbVery high. May need more than one treatment, with a filter clean between.

Common questions

Does this replace chlorine?

No. Chlorine or another sanitizer keeps the water safe. Removing phosphate takes away algae's food, so the chlorine isn't constantly fighting new growth.

Is lanthanum chloride safe to swim with?

Lanthanum chloride phosphate removers are sold for home pools and used widely. We follow the product label for dosing and swim times, and the solid that forms is filtered out.

Why is my water cloudy after treatment?

That haze is the phosphate binding into a solid. Running the filter clears it, and we clean the filter afterward.

How often does phosphate need treating?

It depends on trees, lawn care, fill water and rain. We test every visit and treat when the reading calls for it.

Can you fix a pool that's already green?

Yes. We shock and clean it first to kill the algae, then remove phosphate so it stays clear.
